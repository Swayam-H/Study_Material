Ch 6, Thermodynamics I: gases and the first law

2 min read

Gases (Focus 1)

  • Perfect gas: pV = nRT. Dalton: pi​=xi​p.
  • Kinetic model: pV=31​Nm⟨v2⟩, and ⟨Ek​⟩=23​kT depends only on T.
  • Speeds: vmp​=2RT/M​ < vˉ=8RT/πM​ < vrms​=3RT/M​. N₂ at 300 K: vrms​≈517 m/s. O₂ at 25 °C: vmp​=394 m/s. Speed goes as 1/M​.
  • Mean free path λ=kT/(σp), about 0.1 μm at 1 atm.
  • Real gases: compression factor Z=pVm​/RT. Z < 1 means attractions dominate; Z > 1 means repulsions do. vdW: p=Vm​−bRT​−Vm2​a​.
  • vdW constants: Tc​=8a/27Rb, pc​=a/27b2, Vc​=3b, Boyle temperature TB​=a/Rb, inversion temperature TI​=2a/Rb.

First law (Focus 2)

  • ΔU = q + w, with w the work done on the system, w=−∫pex​dV.
  • Reversible isothermal (perfect gas): w=−nRTln(Vf​/Vi​), ΔU = 0, q = −w.
  • U of a perfect gas depends only on T: ΔU=nCV​ΔT for any path. Also Cp​−CV​=nR. Monatomic: CV​=23​R, γ = 5/3. Diatomic: CV​=25​R, γ = 7/5.
  • H = U + pV, so ΔH=qp​. For reactions, ΔH=ΔU+Δng​RT.
  • Reversible adiabatic: q = 0, pVγ and TVγ−1 are constant, and w=ΔU=CV​ΔT. The adiabat is steeper than the isotherm.
  • Free expansion (perfect gas): q = w = ΔU = 0 and ΔT = 0.
  • Thermochemistry: Hess's law. Δr​H=∑Δf​H(products)−∑Δf​H(reactants), or the reverse order with combustion enthalpies. Kirchhoff: Δr​H(T2​)=Δr​H(T1​)+ΔCp​ΔT.
  • Exact differentials: dz = M dx + N dy is exact if ∂M/∂y=∂N/∂x. dU and dH are exact; dq and dw are not.
  • Joule–Thomson: enthalpy is constant, μJT​=(∂T/∂p)H​. μ > 0 means the gas cools on expansion (below TI​).
