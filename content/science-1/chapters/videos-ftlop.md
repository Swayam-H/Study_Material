# Reference: For the Love of Physics videos

Videos from the channel [For the Love of Physics](https://www.youtube.com/@FortheLoveofPhysics) (Dibyajyoti Das), matched to the 12 endsem topics. The channel is strongest on quantum mechanics and special relativity, which are about two-thirds of the endsem, so they come first here, ahead of the pre-midsem topics.

Titles and lengths were copied from the channel's Videos tab on 21 Nov 2026. Every link goes straight to the video, using the IDs from the channel's Videos tab.
