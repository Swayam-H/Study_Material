Quantum mechanics

2 min read

Schrödinger equation

iℏ∂t∂Ψ​=−2mℏ2​∂x2∂2Ψ​+UΨ​(time-dependent, 1-D)

For U=U(x) separate Ψ=ψ(x)e−iEt/ℏ:

−2mℏ2​dx2d2ψ​+Uψ=Eψ​(steady-state)

Acceptable ψ: continuous, single-valued, normalisable (∫∣Ψ∣2dx=1), with dψ/dx continuous where U is finite. Energy quantisation comes from these conditions alone.

Property Statement
linearity any sum of solutions is a solution, which gives interference
stationary state ∣Ψ∣2=∣ψ∣2, constant in time

Expectation values and operators

⟨x⟩=∫x∣Ψ∣2dx,⟨p⟩=∫Ψ∗(−iℏ∂x∂​)Ψdx,p^​=−iℏ∂x∂​

eikx is an eigenfunction of p^​ with p=ℏk. sinkx is not (it is an equal mix of +ℏk and −ℏk).

Particle in an infinite box (width L)

ψn​=L2​​sinLnπx​,En​=2mL2n2π2ℏ2​=8mL2n2h2​,n=1,2,…​

⟨x⟩=L/2 for every state. Normalisation of ψ=Ax(L−x): A=30/L5​.

3-D cubical box:

E=2mL2π2ℏ2​(n12​+n22​+n32​)

Levels at 3 (1,1,1), 6 (1,1,2) and permutations (3-fold), 9 (1,2,2) and permutations (3-fold), in units of π2ℏ2/2mL2.

Finite well

Inside: ψ=Asinkx+Bcoskx with k=2mE​/ℏ. Outside (E<U): decaying exponentials with

a=ℏ2m(U−E)​​

Join ψ and dψ/dx at each wall. Levels lie below those of an infinite well of the same width because ψ leaks into the walls.

Tunnelling

T≈e−2k2​L,k2​=ℏ2m(U−E)​​​

Doubling the width squares T; a heavier particle (larger m) tunnels far less.

Harmonic oscillator (U=21​kx2)

En​=(n+21​)hν=(n+21​)ℏω,n=0,1,2,…​

Zero-point energy 21​hν; level spacing hν. Near any minimum x0​: U≈U(x0​)+21​U′′(x0​)(x−x0​)2, so k=U′′(x0​).
