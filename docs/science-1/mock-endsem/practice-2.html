Part II

Q17 (6). CV​=23​R=12.47 J/(K mol).

Step (i), constant volume, 300→600 K:

w=0,q=ΔU=CV​ΔT=12.47×300=3.74 kJ​
ΔS=CV​ln300600​=12.47×0.693=8.64 J/K​

Step (ii), isothermal at 600 K, V→2V:

ΔU=0,q=−w=RTln2=8.314×600×0.693=3.46 kJ​,w=−3.46 kJ
ΔS=Rln2=5.76 J/K​

(b) Totals.

q=7.20 kJ,w=−3.46 kJ,ΔU=3.74 kJ,ΔS=14.4 J/K

Check: q+w=7.20−3.46=3.74 kJ =ΔU ✓.

U and S are state functions: they depend only on the end states, so every path between them gives the same ΔU and ΔS. Heat and work are path functions: a different path (for example expand first, then heat) gives different q and w with the same sum q+w.

  • (a) 1 for each of: step (i) q,w,ΔU; step (i) ΔS; step (ii) q,w,ΔU; step (ii) ΔS. (b) 1 for the totals and check, 1 for the state-function explanation.
