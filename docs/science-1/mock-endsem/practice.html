Practice

Mock endsem

5 min read

Time: 120 minutes. Total: 50 points. A simple calculator is allowed. No formula sheet. Sit it in one go, on paper, before you open the solutions page.

Layout. Modelled on the 2025 endsem (120 minutes, 50 points, calculator allowed, no formula sheet, short questions then longer ones). Part I is 16 short questions of 2 points each (32 points). Part II is 3 longer questions of 6 points each (18 points). The questions run through the 12 chapters in order, so you can see which chapters you lose marks in.

Constants

me​=9.11×10−31 kg,h=6.626×10−34 J s,kB​=1.381×10−23 J/K,c=3×108 m/s
R=8.314 J K−1mol−1,hc=1240 eV nm,ℏc=197 eV nm,me​c2=0.511 MeV,EnH​=−13.6/n2 eV

Part I: short questions (2 points each)

Q1 (Chapter 1). The logistic map xn+1​=rxn​(1−xn​) has r=2.5. Find the non-zero fixed point and decide whether it is stable.

Q2 (Chapter 2). A rocket in free space burns fuel with exhaust speed 2 km/s relative to the rocket. What mass ratio m0​/m is needed to reach 5 km/s from rest?

Q3 (Chapter 3). An Atwood machine has masses m1​=3m and m2​=m on a light string over a light pulley. Write the Lagrangian in one coordinate and find the acceleration.

Q4 (Chapter 4). Two stars of equal mass M orbit each other in circles about their centre of mass, a distance d apart. Find the orbital period.

Q5 (Chapter 5). A rod of proper length 1 m flies lengthwise past you at 0.6c. How long does it appear to you, and how long does it take to pass a fixed point?

Q6 (Chapter 5). An electron has kinetic energy 0.511 MeV. Find its γ, its speed and its momentum in MeV/c.

Q7 (Chapter 6). Two moles of a perfect gas expand reversibly and isothermally at 300 K from 10 L to 40 L. Find q, w and ΔU.

Q8 (Chapter 6). A monatomic perfect gas at 300 K is compressed reversibly and adiabatically to half its volume. Find the final temperature.

Q9 (Chapter 7). One mole of ice melts at 273 K and 1 atm, with Δfus​H=6.01 kJ/mol. Find ΔS of the system, of the surroundings, and in total.

Q10 (Chapter 7). For N2​+3H2​→2NH3​, ΔH∘=−92.2 kJ and ΔS∘=−198.7 J/K. Find ΔG∘ at 298 K and the temperature above which the reaction stops being spontaneous.

Q11 (Chapter 8). Light of wavelength 400 nm falls on a metal with work function 2.3 eV. Find the maximum kinetic energy of the photoelectrons and the stopping potential.

Q12 (Chapter 9). Find the de Broglie wavelength of an electron accelerated from rest through 150 V.

Q13 (Chapter 10). Find the wavelength of the photon emitted when a He+ ion drops from n=3 to n=2.

Q14 (Chapter 11). An electron is confined to a one-dimensional box 0.5 nm wide. Find E1​ and the wavelength of the photon emitted in the n=2→1 transition.

Q15 (Chapter 11). An electron with energy 2 eV meets a barrier 5 eV high and 0.4 nm wide. Estimate the transmission probability.

Q16 (Chapter 12). For the 3d state of hydrogen, give n, l, the possible ml​, the magnitude of L, the number of radial nodes, and the only allowed single-photon decay.

Part II: longer questions (6 points each)

Q17 (Chapter 6, 7). One mole of a monatomic perfect gas (CV,m​=23​R) is taken through two reversible steps: (i) heated at constant volume from 300 K to 600 K; (ii) expanded isothermally at 600 K until its volume doubles.

(a) For each step find q, w, ΔU and ΔS of the system. (4 points)

(b) Give the totals for the two steps and explain why ΔS and ΔU would be the same along any other path between the same end states, but q and w would not. (2 points)

Q18 (Chapters 3, 4). A particle of mass m moves in a plane with the Lagrangian

L=21​m(r˙2+r2θ˙2)−21​kr2

(a) Which quantities are conserved, and why? (2 points)

(b) Find the conjugate momenta and the Hamiltonian, and write Hamilton's equations. (2 points)

(c) For a circular orbit with angular momentum ℓ, find the radius and the angular frequency. Does the frequency depend on the radius? (2 points)

Q19 (Chapters 11, 12).

(a) A particle in a box 0<x<L is in the state ψ=Asin(2πx/L). Normalise it. (1 point)

(b) Find the probability of finding it in the left third of the box, 0<x<L/3. (2 points)

(c) If the particle is an electron and L=1 nm, find the wavelength of the photon emitted when it drops from this state to the ground state. (1 point)

(d) A hydrogen wave function is ψ∝re−r/2a0​sinθe−iφ. Find n, l and ml​. (2 points)
