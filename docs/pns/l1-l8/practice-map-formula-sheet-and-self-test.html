2. Formula sheet

Sets and axioms (L1, L2)

  • De Morgan: (⋃Ai​)c=⋂Aic​, (⋂Ai​)c=⋃Aic​.
  • σ-algebra: Ω∈F; closed under complement and countable union (hence countable intersection, difference).
  • Axioms: P(Ω)=1, P≥0, countable additivity for disjoint events.
  • P(Ac)=1−P(A); A⊆B⇒P(A)≤P(B) and P(B∖A)=P(B)−P(A).
  • P(A∪B)=P(A)+P(B)−P(AB); three sets: +singles −pairs +triple.
  • Union bound: P(⋃Ai​)≤∑P(Ai​). Bonferroni: P(⋂Ai​)≥∑P(Ai​)−(n−1).
  • Continuity: An​↑A or An​↓A⇒P(An​)→P(A).
  • P(A)=0 does not mean A=∅.

Conditioning and independence (L3)

P(B∣A)=P(A)P(AB)​,P(A1​⋯An​)=P(A1​)P(A2​∣A1​)⋯P(An​∣A1​⋯An−1​)
P(A)=i∑​P(A∣Bi​)P(Bi​),P(Bj​∣A)=∑i​P(A∣Bi​)P(Bi​)P(A∣Bj​)P(Bj​)​
  • Independent: P(AB)=P(A)P(B); then also (A,Bc), (Ac,B), (Ac,Bc).
  • Mutual independence: product rule for every subcollection. Pairwise does not imply mutual.
  • Disjoint with positive probabilities implies dependent. P(E)=0 or 1: independent of everything.
  • Conditional independence: P(AB∣C)=P(A∣C)P(B∣C). Neither implies nor is implied by independence.
  • At least one of independent events: 1−∏(1−P(Ai​)). Series: multiply working probabilities. Parallel: multiply failure probabilities.

3. Exam-style self-test

Try these under exam conditions (about 30 minutes), then check the answers.

  1. Ω={1,2,3,4}. List the σ-algebra generated by {1} and {2}. How many sets does it have?
  2. P(A)=0.5, P(B)=0.4, P(A∣B)=0.3. Find P(A∪B), P(B∣A) and P(Ac∩Bc). Are A and B independent?
  3. A test has sensitivity 0.95 (P(+∣D)) and specificity 0.90 (P(−∣Dc)). Prevalence is 5%. Find P(D∣+).
  4. X∼ Geometric(31​). Find P(X>5∣X>2) and P(X is even).
  5. X∼Exp(2) and Y=X​. Find fY​.
  6. X∼N(1,4). Find P(∣X∣<1).
  7. X has CDF F(x)=1−x−2 for x≥1 (and 0 otherwise). Find fX​, E[X], Var(X) and P(X>2∣X>1.5).
  8. U∼U(0,1). Find the distribution of Y=−2ln(1−U) and its mean.

Answers

Answers
  1. The atoms are {1}, {2}, {3,4}, so the σ-algebra is all 23=8 unions of atoms: ∅, {1}, {2}, {3,4}, {1,2}, {1,3,4}, {2,3,4}, Ω.
  2. P(AB)=0.3×0.4=0.12. P(A∪B)=0.78, P(B∣A)=0.24, P(AcBc)=0.22. Not independent: 0.12=0.2 (negatively correlated).
  3. 0.95×0.05+0.10×0.950.95×0.05​=0.14250.0475​=31​.
  4. Memoryless: P(X>3)=(32​)3=278​. P(even)=∑j≥1​(32​)2j−131​=31​⋅1−4/92/3​=52​.
  5. g(x)=x​ is increasing on [0,∞); h(y)=y2, h′=2y: fY​(y)=2e−2y2⋅2y=4ye−2y2 for y>0.
  6. P(−1<X<1)=Φ(0)−Φ(−1)=0.5−0.1587=0.3413.
  7. fX​(x)=2x−3 for x≥1. E[X]=∫1∞​2x−2dx=2. E[X2]=∫1∞​2x−1dx=∞, so the variance is infinite. P(X>2∣X>1.5)=1/2.251/4​=0.5625.
