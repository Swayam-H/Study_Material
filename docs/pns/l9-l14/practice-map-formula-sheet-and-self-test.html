3. Exam-style self-test

Try these without notes (about 40 minutes), then check the answers below.

T1. The joint PMF of X∈{0,1} and Y∈{0,1,2} is p(0,0)=0.1, p(0,1)=0.2, p(0,2)=0.1, p(1,0)=0.2, p(1,1)=0.1, p(1,2)=0.3. Find the marginals, E[X∣Y=2] and Cov(X,Y). Are they independent?

T2. X∼ Exp(1) and Y=min(X,2). Is Y discrete, continuous or mixed? Find P(Y=2) and E[Y].

T3. fXY​(x,y)=c(x+y) for 0≤x≤y≤1. Find c, fX​ and P(X+Y≤1).

T4. X, Y i.i.d. Exp(λ). Find P(X>2Y).

T5. The number of customers in a day is Poisson(4), and each spends an amount uniform on [0,100], independently. Find the mean and standard deviation of the day's takings.

T6. A coin's bias X is uniform on [0,1]. You toss it once and get heads. Find the posterior density of X and the probability that the next toss is heads.

T7. X∼U[0,1] and Y∼ Exp(1) are independent. Find the PDF of X+Y.

T8. X1​,X2​,X3​ each have variance 1, and Cov(Xi​,Xj​)=0.5 for i=j. Find Var(X1​+X2​+X3​) and Var(X1​−X2​).

T9. N∼ Geometric(41​). Find E[N∣N>3] and P(N=6∣N>3).

T10. True or false? (a) If Cov(X,Y)=0, then Var(X+Y)=Var(X)+Var(Y). (b) E[X∣Y] is a number. (c) If X and Y are independent, E[X∣Y]=E[X]. (d) For any random variable, FX​(x)−FX​(x−)=P(X=x). (e) fXY​(x,y)=6xy2 on [0,1]2 makes X and Y independent. (f) fXY​(x,y)=8xy on 0≤x≤y≤1 makes X and Y independent. (g) For independent X and Y, Var(X−Y)=Var(X)−Var(Y).

Answers

Answers

T1. pX​=(0.4,0.6), pY​=(0.3,0.3,0.4). E[X∣Y=2]=0.40.3​=0.75. E[X]=0.6, E[Y]=0.3+0.8=1.1, E[XY]=1⋅0.1+2⋅0.3=0.7, so Cov=0.7−0.66=0.04. Dependent: p(0,0)=0.1=0.4⋅0.3.

T2. Mixed: a point mass P(Y=2)=P(X≥2)=e−2≈0.135 plus density e−y on (0,2). E[Y]=∫02​P(X>t)dt=1−e−2≈0.865.

T3. ∫01​∫0y​(x+y)dxdy=∫01​23​y2dy=21​, so c=2. fX​(x)=∫x1​2(x+y)dy=1+2x−3x2 on [0,1]. For P(X+Y≤1), x runs over [0,21​] and y over [x,1−x]; the inner integral is 1−4x2, and ∫01/2​(1−4x2)dx=31​.

T4. Condition on Y: ∫0∞​e−2λyλe−λydy=31​.

T5. Random sum: mean 4⋅50=200; variance E[N]Var(X)+(E[X])2Var(N)=4⋅12104​+2500⋅4=340000​, so the SD is about 115.5.

T6. Posterior ∝x⋅1, so fX∣H​(x)=2x on [0,1]. P(H2​∣H1​)=E[X∣H1​]=∫01​2x2dx=32​ (Laplace's rule of succession with one observation).

T7. For 0≤z≤1: ∫0z​e−(z−x)dx=1−e−z. For z>1: ∫01​e−(z−x)dx=(e−1)e−z. Zero for z<0.

T8. 3+2⋅3⋅0.5=6, and 1+1−2⋅0.5=1.

T9. Memorylessness: N−3∣N>3∼ Geom(41​), so E[N∣N>3]=3+4=7 and P(N=6∣N>3)=P(N=3)=(43​)241​=649​.

T10. (a) True. (b) False: it is a random variable, a function of Y. (c) True. (d) True (L9 slide 3). (e) True: 6xy2=2x⋅3y2 on a square. (f) False: the support is a triangle. (g) False: variances add, Var(X−Y)=Var(X)+Var(Y).
